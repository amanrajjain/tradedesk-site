SMS Consent / Call to Action

Last updated: March 2026

How end users consent to receive messages

End users opt in by calling the local business’s published phone number to request service. Before any SMS is sent, the caller hears the following automated disclosure during the call:

“Thank you for calling [Business Name]. If we miss your call, you may receive up to 3 text messages related to your inquiry, including a missed-call response and scheduling follow-up. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out and HELP for help. By continuing this call, you agree to receive these messages from [Business Name]. Consent is not a condition of purchase.”

SMS is sent only if the business does not answer the call after this disclosure is played. Messages are sent only to the same phone number that placed the call.

No purchased lists, no web lead forms, and no third-party lead data are used for this campaign.

Program purpose

This messaging program is used only to respond to inbound callers requesting service from a local home-service business.

Message frequency

1 initial missed-call response plus up to 2 follow-up scheduling messages per inquiry. Maximum 3 messages per call event. This is not a recurring subscription program.

Sample messages

Sample message 1:

Hi, this is Mike’s Plumbing. Sorry we missed your call. You can book a service time here: cal.com/tradedesk/service-appointment. Reply STOP to opt out, HELP for help. Msg & data rates may apply.

Sample message 2:

Hi, this is Mike’s Plumbing following up on your call. We have Thursday at 2 PM or Friday at 9 AM available. Reply with your preferred time or book here: cal.com/tradedesk/service-appointment. Reply STOP to opt out, HELP for help. Msg & data rates may apply.

Support

For support, reply HELP to any message or contact hello@tradedesk.co.
